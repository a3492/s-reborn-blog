The following is a fictional scenario for illustration.

Kyung's team gathered opening shots for a tumbler advertisement. The product was the same, but the background, colors and camera motion differed in every shot. A colleague asked which was better. Kyung could not answer: before asking who liked a shot, the team needed to identify **what difference it was comparing**.

**In one sentence:** Keep the product image and brand conditions fixed and vary only camera motion to create review candidates.

## You can compare only when you can identify what changed

An advertising hook is the opening scene that attracts attention. If the camera in B moves closer and the product changes shape, first check whether both shots depict the same product. When color, copy, duration and product change together, it is difficult to identify the cause of the apparent difference.

Kyung used the same approved image and copy for both versions and recorded the single variable. Even when only one input changes, multiple aspects of a generated result may change. Some results must be excluded.

## Record the fixed conditions first

Record the approved image, navy background, aspect ratio, displayed copy and duration. The example product is an ordinary tumbler. The [official Higgsfield DoP guide](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/ai-models/how-do-i-use-dop) describes specifying camera motion when generating a draft from an input image. Changing models or presets may change other aspects, so examine the actual outputs.

| Criterion | A | B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed conditions | Same shot, image, color and copy | Same as A |
| Variable | Maintain front-facing view | Move slowly closer |
| Review | Is the product visible in the opening? | Are background color, shape and copy placement preserved? |

## Separate draft review from campaign deployment

1. Place the candidates' first frames side by side at the same size. If color, the product label or shape has changed, regenerate or exclude that draft.
2. Apply identical copy during editing. Reviewers answer predetermined questions such as whether the product is recognizable. A selected draft is a pre-release candidate, not evidence of advertising performance.
3. For health-related material, do not generate efficacy or outcome claims. Send final text and imagery through the organization's approval process before deployment.

Kyung's team found that B's label was warped and excluded it. They kept their favorable impression of the motion on record, but preserving the same product became the first comparison criterion.

## Pause in the middle even if the first frame looks identical

In the fictional example, A retains the front-facing view and B moves slowly closer. Compare corresponding points at the beginning, middle and end. Pause and check identifiable parts such as the label, lid and body to separate camera approach from deformation of the label.

- **Accept:** The tumbler appears larger in B, but the label retains its shape. If the product remains recognizable and copy placement is unobstructed, keep it for internal review.
- **Exclude:** The logo warps or the label changes only in the middle. Exclude even if the first and last frames look fine; record where and why.
- **Record:** "Same image and copy / camera motion only / label warped in middle / B on hold." The next reviewer can revisit the same scene.

The output is a candidate video for team review. Viewer conversion and actual campaign performance are still unknown. To practice separating motion from composition, see [the Runway exercise (Korean)](/blog/ai-tools-general/runway-gen-video-overview/).
